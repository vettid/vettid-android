VettID is the app for your VettID vault: a personal vault in a hardware-isolated AWS Nitro Enclave that only you can open. The app holds your Protean Credential and is the only app that can unlock your vault.

You need a VettID membership. Get a setup code on the VettID account site, scan its QR code (or type it), then choose a vault PIN and a credential password. You can also move VettID from your old phone, or recover your vault on a new one if you kept the sealed backup of your credential.

* Connections: invite people with a link or QR code and check a safety code together. Services can be connections too.
* Messages with your connections, with photos.
* Approvals: requests that need your decision.
* Vault: logins, documents and other secrets, organized by tags. You choose what each connection can see.
* Location: share with a connection once or for a set time, at the precision you choose. Sharing starts only from your tap and shows a notification while it runs. The app never asks for background location. A shared position can be shown on a map from OpenFreeMap or a server you set, after you agree for each server.
* Presence, votes from services, History, and devices: pair an AI agent or a desktop, with only the access you approve.
* An optional app lock, and a daily check of your PIN and credential password.

Privacy:
* What the app sends to your vault is end-to-end encrypted to it. Your connections' vaults talk to your vault, never to your phone.
* The app checks your vault's enclave attestation against VettID's published releases.
* VettID's relay cannot read what it carries; it sees timing and sizes. VettID's account service knows your account and when your vault is unlocked.
* No position is stored on the phone.
* Screenshots of VettID are blocked unless you allow them, for at most an hour.

This build contains no Google software. Notifications come through VettID's on-phone service, which keeps its own connection to your vault. Calls and the Bitcoin wallet are not included.

This app is signed only with VettID's own key, which Google never holds; your vault accepts only apps signed with a key it knows. F-Droid builds the same source and publishes VettID's signed APK only if the two match apart from the signature.
